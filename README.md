# Rip City Tycoon

Rip City Tycoon is an economy spin-off of [Rip City](https://github.com/illuminax13/pokemon-pack-simulator). You get the same pack opening, foils, binders and pull rates, but every pack costs money. You start with **$500**. You buy packs and boxes at real MSRP or vintage market prices, sell the cards you pull, and earn cash from daily and weekly challenges, achievements and a daily login bonus.

Live: https://illuminax13.github.io/rip-city-tycoon/

## Economy
- **Wallet:** starts at $500 and is shown in the header. It is saved in localStorage as `rctEcon`.
- **Prices:** in-print sets (2025 and later) cost MSRP ($4.49 for Pokémon, $5.99 for Lorcana). Older and vintage packs use a market-based price capped at $199.99. Booster boxes are 20% off the per-pack price, ETBs 10% and bundles 8%. Prices are generated by `python3 tools/build_sealed.py --shop`.
- **Selling:** sell from the pack summary, the binder, or the zoom view (button, or swipe up on the card). Cards are priced from live or snapshot market prices, with a bulk floor by rarity. Sales of $25 or more ask you to confirm. The hub has "sell all duplicates" and "sell all bulk".
- **Challenges:** 3 daily and 3 weekly challenges, chosen deterministically from the America/Chicago date (weeks start on Monday).
- **Achievements:** all 75 pay cash, scaled by tier (Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum).
- **Safety net:** a daily login bonus with a streak, plus a once-a-day $25 bailout when you're broke and have nothing worth selling.
- The wallet tab shows your transaction history and net worth (cash plus collection value).

Storage keys use the `rct*` prefix. The service worker caches are `rct-*` and the scope is `/rip-city-tycoon/`, so it never collides with the original Rip City on the same origin.
